About FilAus

A shared home for stories that travel between islands.

FilAus exists to celebrate Filipino heritage in Australia and strengthen the relationships that help our community belong, contribute, and be heard.

01

Connecting Communities

Fostering a sense of belonging among Filipinos in Australia through shared experiences and support.

02

Celebrating Culture

Highlighting Filipino traditions, arts, and history—and the ways they enrich Australian life.

03

Empowering Voices

Giving Filipino Australians space to share their stories, talents, perspectives, and experiences.
